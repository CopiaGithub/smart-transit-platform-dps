# Transit Display Platform

Automated school bus boarding display solution for **Delhi Public School, Nerul, Navi Mumbai**, designed to replace the manual WhatsApp and blackboard process with synchronized outdoor and indoor video walls.

## Client context

- **School:** Delhi Public School, Nerul, Navi Mumbai
- **Transport:** Outsourced to one bus contractor with a fleet of ~50 buses (~45 in daily service)
- **Students:** Primary through 12th standard
- **Typical pickup/drop window:** 12:45 pm – 2:30 pm

### Campus layout

- Buses enter through **Gate 6** into a large U-shaped compound and exit through **Gate 1**
- About **25 buses** can line up at a time; there is no overtaking (first in, first out)
- **Stations 1–23** are marked from the boarding point toward the gate entrance, spaced at roughly one bus length
- Students leave the building from **two exits** and board buses within a ~15-minute window after school ends

## Current modus operandi (manual)

1. Entrance security posts each arriving bus number to the teachers’ WhatsApp group
2. Teachers write the bus → station assignment on blackboards at both building exits
3. Students read the boards, find their station, and board with help from class teachers
4. Buses enter in random order, so station assignments change throughout the boarding window
5. If a bus breaks down, the contractor may send a reserve bus at the last minute and change the windshield number

This process is chaotic day to day. Teachers must stand outdoors in monsoon and summer weather to manage boarding.

## Proposed solution

InfoLED Networks LLP is providing an automated display system:

| Display | Location | Approx. size | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoor video wall | Near entrance gate | 8 × 8 ft | Live bus → station mapping as buses enter |
| Indoor video wall | Building exit 1 | 4 × 6 ft | Same live mapping for students and teachers |
| Indoor video wall | Building exit 2 | 4 × 6 ft | Same live mapping for students and teachers |

## Problems this platform addresses

- Real-time visibility of which bus is at which station
- Synchronized information at the entrance and both building exits
- Last-minute reserve bus / substitution updates without relying on WhatsApp or blackboards
- Reduced teacher load and less chaotic boarding
